# 🏥 Hospital Performance & Health Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This project presents an end-to-end interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze hospital operational metrics, patient demographics, clinical outcomes, and financial performance. Using structured healthcare data, this solution translates raw administrative metrics into actionable insights to assist healthcare managers, department heads, and executives in optimizing patient flow, bed utilization, and financial performance.

---

## 📸 Key Dashboards & Visualizations

### 1. Executive Summary
Provides a high-level overview of critical KPI metrics, including total patient volume, average treatment costs, overall length of stay (LOS), and key departmental indicators.

### 2. Patient Demographics & Admission Trends
Displays patient volume distributions broken down by age groups, gender, and admission types (Emergency, Elective, Urgent), along with temporal admission trends.
![Patient Demographics](Screenshot%202026-09-24%20030250.png)

### 3. Departmental Performance & Bed Utilization
Tracks room occupancy, bed turnover rates, and average stay durations across specialized departments (e.g., ICU, Surgery, General Ward, Emergency).
![Departmental Performance](Screenshot%202026-09-24%20030313.png)

### 4. Financial & Billing Analysis
Analyzes total generated revenue, average cost per treatment specialty, insurance claim settlement statuses, and outstanding balance metrics.

### 5. Patient Outcomes & Clinical Quality Insights

## 📊 Key Findings & Strategic Insights

* **Admission Pressure:** Emergency cases form the highest proportion of sudden intake spikes, highlighting the need for dynamic shift allocation and streamlined triage processes during peak hours.
* **Length of Stay (LOS) Impact:** Intensive care and surgical units consistently exhibit longer average stay durations, directly driving higher billing totals and requiring focused discharge planning.
* **Financial Revenue Drivers:** Complex specialized treatments contribute to the majority of total hospital revenue, while delayed insurance claim settlements account for primary cash flow bottlenecks.
* **Bed Capacity Management:** Bed occupancy rates fluctuate significantly between weekdays and weekends, presenting opportunities to optimize bed assignment protocols in general wards.
